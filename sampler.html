Contrast (Minimum)

Text needs a contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1 against its background. Large text, 24px or 18.66px bold and up, needs 3:1.

Who it helps
People with low vision or colour vision deficiency, and anyone reading a phone outdoors.
Common failure
Light grey placeholder text, and white labels on bright brand colours.
Fix
Darken the text until the ratio reads 4.5:1 or more. Logos and disabled controls are exempt.

Keyboard

Everything a mouse can do, a keyboard must be able to do. And wherever the keyboard can go in, it must be able to get back out.

These on-screen keys drive a pretend focus ring, so the demo works on a phone and never traps your real keyboard.

Who it helps
People who cannot use a mouse, screen reader users, switch users and keyboard-first power users.
Common failure
A <div> with a click handler, and embedded widgets or modals that swallow Tab.
Fix
Use real <button> and <a> elements. Let Esc close overlays and hand focus back.

Focus Visible

When someone moves through a page by keyboard, they must be able to see which element has focus. It is their cursor.

Who it helps
Sighted keyboard users, including people with tremor, RSI or a broken trackpad.
Common failure
:focus { outline: none } added to tidy a design, with nothing put in its place.
Fix
:focus-visible { outline: 2px solid; outline-offset: 2px } in a colour with 3:1 contrast.
2.5.8
Level AAOperable · new in WCAG 2.2

Target Size (Minimum)

Anything you tap or click needs to be at least 24 by 24 CSS pixels, or have enough empty space around it to make up the difference.

Bench · tap the filled square

0Hits
0Misses
–Accuracy

Use a thumb if you are on a phone. Small targets can still pass when a 24px circle centred on each one touches no neighbour. The AAA criterion, 2.5.5, asks for 44px.

Who it helps
People with tremor or limited dexterity, and anyone using a phone one-handed on a bus.
Common failure
Tightly packed icon buttons, tiny close crosses, and pagination dots.
Fix
Pad the control to 24px or more. The icon can stay small while its clickable area grows.
